# Code of Conduct

DenseFrame contributors are expected to keep collaboration respectful, focused, and practical.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct without being hostile.
- Assume good intent while still asking for evidence.
- Keep discussions technical, specific, and actionable.
- Respect privacy and do not pressure people to share raw scans or private device data.
- Follow the local-first and no-hidden-network principles of the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated off-topic arguments that block project work.
- Attempts to add hidden tracking, analytics, credential collection, or deceptive behavior.

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove comments and contributions that violate these expectations.
